---

## 🎯 Pedagogical Objectives & Evaluation Targets
1. **Total Physical Response (TPR) & Classroom Energy:**
   - Menghidupkan kelas Zoom dalam 60 detik pertama menggunakan instruksi fisik (*"raise hand high"*, *"gamer smile"*, *"gamer claws"*).
   - Menunjukkan antusiasme tinggi, ramah, dan sabar khas guru Timedoor.
2. **Concept Demystification:**
   - Menjelaskan percabangan logika (*IF = True condition*, *ELSE = Fallback plan*) melalui analogi dunia nyata (Cuaca hujan/cerah) dan analogi lucu (*The River Dilemma: apa yang terjadi jika tidak ada ELSE?*).
3. **Visual & Block Coding Connection:**
   - Menghubungkan logika verbal ke bentuk visual balok Scratch asli (*The Hungry C-Block*).
4. **Interactive Gamification:**
   - Permainan tebak kartu misteri 2D (*Flip Cards*) untuk menguji pemahaman murid secara partisipatif.
5. **Strict Time Management (05:00 Target):**
   - Tepat 1 menit per slide, diakhiri dengan perayaan (*Level 5 Unlocked*) dan *Virtual High-Five*.

## 🎙️ Naskah Mengajar Kata demi Kata (Word-for-Word Spoken English Script)

### 🟡 SLIDE 1: Welcome & The Brain Code Game (00:00 – 01:00)
* **Visual:** Maskot Pixel the Bear (Kenney 2D) melompat ramah + 2 kotak tantangan reaksi otak.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Good afternoon, my awesome super-coders! How is everyone doing today? Awesome!*
  > 
  > *Today, we are embarking on a top-secret quest: **Mission Save Pixel the Bear!** Pixel needs our brain superpower to make decisions!*
  > 
  > *Before we start, let's play a super quick brain game! Listen closely to my rules:*
  > * 👉 **IF** you are wearing something blue today... **raise your hand up high!** 🙋‍♂️
  > * 👉 **ELSE** (if you're not wearing blue like me!)... **give me a big friendly wave!** 👋
  > *(Pause 2 detik, senyum, beri apresiasi: 'Awesome! Look at those waves and hands! That is IF and ELSE in action!')*
  > 
  > * 👉 Now... **IF** you love playing video games like Minecraft or Roblox... **give me a big, big smile!** 😁
  > 
  > *Do you know what just happened? Your brain just executed a secret superpower called **CONDITIONALS!**"*

---

### 🔵 SLIDE 2: Real-Life Logic — The Weather Dilemma (01:00 – 02:00)
* **Visual:** Kartu Hujan (*Panda with Umbrella*) vs Kartu Cerah (*Duck with Sunglasses*).
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Now, how do computers and robots make decisions? They do it just like us every single day!*
  > 
  > *Imagine looking outside your bedroom window in the morning:*
  > * 👉 **IF** it is raining outside... what do you grab? That's right, an **umbrella!** ☂️
  > * 👉 **ELSE**... if it's sunny outside... what do you wear? Cool **sunglasses!** 😎
  > 
  > *In coding, **IF** checks if a condition is **TRUE**. And **ELSE** is our superhero backup plan when the condition is **FALSE**!"*

---

### 🟢 SLIDE 3: Connecting to Scratch — The Super C-Block (02:00 – 03:15)
* **Visual:** Gambar balok Scratch 2D asli (`if path ahead? then walk (10) steps else jump over river!`).
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Now let's bring this superpower into **Scratch!** Look at this giant yellow block with a hungry mouth — we call it the **C-Block!***
  > 
  > *Look at Pixel's code:*
  > * `when green flag clicked`
  > * `IF path ahead?` ➔ `THEN walk 10 steps! 🐾`
  > * `ELSE` ➔ `JUMP over the river! 🚀`
  > 
  > *Super-coders, here is a big question:*
  > ***What would happen if we FORGOT the ELSE block when Pixel reached the river?***
  > *(Tunggu respon: 'He falls into the water!')*
  > *Exactly! Splash! He would walk right into the river!*
  > *That is why **ELSE** is the real hero — it is the backup plan that saves our robot!"*

---

### 🟣 SLIDE 4: Interactive Challenge — Mystery Card Quest (03:15 – 04:15)
* **Visual:** Dua kartu terbalik (Card A & Card B). Saat diklik/dibuka, Card A = 9 of Hearts (Merah), Card B = 7 of Spades (Biru).
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Time for our Mystery Card Challenge! Who wants to pick first?*
  > 
  > *Here is our code rule:*
  > * 👉 **IF** the card is **RED** ➔ Draw 2 cards! 🎴
  > * 👉 **ELSE** (if it's not red) ➔ Do 3 super jumps! 🦘
  > 
  > *Let's check Card A... Look, it's **RED!** The condition is TRUE! What do we do? We draw 2 cards!*
  > *Now let's flip Card B... it's **BLUE!** The condition is FALSE! So what superpower do we activate? The **ELSE** superpower: Let's do 3 jumps! One, two, three!"*

---

### 🏆 SLIDE 5: Mission Accomplished & Gold Stars (04:15 – 05:00)
* **Visual:** Bintang emas berkilau (*Kenney Gold Star*), Level 5 Unlocked, 2 Golden Rules.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Look at that! We reached Level 5, and Pixel the Bear made it safely across the island!*
  > 
  > *Let's lock in our 2 golden superpowers today:*
  > * 1️⃣ **IF** is the Question: Is this TRUE or FALSE?
  > * 2️⃣ **ELSE** is the Hero Backup Plan: What to do when it's NOT true!
  > 
  > *You all did an incredible job today, super-coders! Can I get a virtual high-five right into the camera? ✋*
  > *(Gerakkan tangan ke webcam)*
  > *BAM! Fantastic job! Thank you everyone, and keep coding like champions!"*
